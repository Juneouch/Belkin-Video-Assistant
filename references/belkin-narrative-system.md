# Belkin Narrative System v1

## Purpose

This reference converts Belkin product categories into narrative decisions. It is a standalone Belkin-native narrative system.

## Product Narrative Systems

### Energy Continuity

For power banks, wall chargers, cables, and wireless chargers. The story is not electricity as spectacle; it is continuity of a human plan. Use movement, battery pressure, a charging ritual, or a calm return to activity. Product proof must remain physically credible.

### Desk Ecosystem

For docks, hubs, adapters, computer accessories, and tablet accessories. The story is the reduction of connection friction. Build from a complex device environment to a legible working system. Show only approved ports, connections, displays, and compatibility.

### Personal Immersion

For audio, headphones, and wireless microphones. The story is the boundary between public space and personal attention, or between creator and audience. Use sound-off-readable visual behavior, then add only approved audio direction.

### Protection and Confidence

For screen protectors and protection accessories. The story is preserving an everyday gesture: touch, carry, look, or use. Avoid fear-based damage imagery unless the brief and product evidence approve it.

### Mobile Play

For Nintendo Switch and gaming accessories. The story is play continuing across location changes. Use travel, social play, and portable transitions without inventing device compatibility or performance.

### Creator Flow

For content-creation accessories, tripods, mounts, and microphones. The story is turning setup friction into a repeatable creative flow. Show the user's intent, device relationship, and output context; do not claim production quality that the product cannot prove.

## Belkin Narrative Models

Use one primary model and optionally one secondary model:

1. **Stay Connected** - the user's activity continues because the product supports continuity.
2. **Remove Friction** - a cluttered or interrupted workflow becomes simple and legible.
3. **Follow the User** - the product travels through several believable moments.
4. **One Product, Many Moments** - one product supports a sequence of related uses.
5. **From Chaos to Control** - visual complexity resolves into an organized system.
6. **Invisible Infrastructure** - the product enables the experience without becoming a gimmick.
7. **Personal Space** - the product creates focus, privacy, comfort, or immersion.
8. **Everyday Continuity** - a familiar ritual becomes more dependable and desirable.

## Category-to-World Matrix

| Category | Default world | Product proof |
|---|---|---|
| Power Bank | transit, travel, festival, outdoor movement | battery/charging state only when approved |
| Wireless Charger | office, bedside, living room | placement, alignment, approved charging indicator |
| Audio | street, transit, bedroom, creator space | wear/use, fit, microphone use, approved audio behavior |
| Dock / Hub | desk, studio, meeting room | approved device and port relationships |
| Switch Accessories | living room, travel, social play | carry, setup, protection, approved compatibility |
| Cables | desk, bag, charging ritual | bend, connection, organization; no invented durability |
| Screen Protector | device setup, touch ritual | clean touch surface and installation only when approved |
| Creator Accessories | creator desk, podcast, live stream | mount, framing, microphone, or workflow relation |
