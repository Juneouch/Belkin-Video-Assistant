# Belkin Aesthetic System v1

## Core Aesthetic: Calm Technology

Belkin visual direction should feel useful, precise, human, and quietly premium. Avoid generic future-tech spectacle. Every visual effect must clarify product form, user behavior, or benefit.

```yaml
material_behavior: "reference-supported surface response"
color_arc: "neutral start -> approved functional accent -> emotional shift -> end frame"
match_cut: "shape | movement | color | light | texture | continuity | hard_cut"
```

## Two-World Visual System

### Product World

- controlled studio or CG environment;
- accurate silhouette, color, material, port layout, cable state, and logo treatment;
- macro detail, edge light, negative fill, motivated reflection, and restrained depth of field;
- camera movement reveals form, state, connection, or proof;
- pure black, saturated neon, energy explosions, and invented interface behavior require explicit approval.

### Brand World

- recognizable everyday environment selected by product category;
- human-scale movement and natural device use;
- product appears as part of the user's action, not as a pasted graphic;
- environment supports the product's benefit without competing with it;
- lighting remains believable across product and lifestyle shots.

## Visual Grammar

| Element | Belkin rule |
|---|---|
| Camera | Use a move only when it reveals, follows, contrasts, or escalates product meaning |
| Light | Use motivated direction, edge separation, material response, and controlled contrast |
| Material | Use only reference-supported materials; encode surface behavior, not invented specification |
| Color arc | Define neutral start, functional accent, emotional shift, and ending lockup |
| Focus | Move focus from environment to product when the product becomes narratively important |
| Motion | Show real weight, cable behavior, device contact, and hand interaction |
| Typography | Reserve readable copy, logo, UI, and legal text for approved design/compositing |

## Match-Cut Library

- **Shape:** user or object rotation to product rotation.
- **Movement:** hand sweep, cable line, train motion, or camera pan into product motion.
- **Color:** environment accent into approved product or graphic accent.
- **Light:** practical light sweep into edge highlight or charging indicator.
- **Texture:** fabric, road, glass, desk, or brushed surface into product material detail.
- **Continuity:** same device, cable direction, hand, orientation, and lighting direction across the cut.

Each storyboard transition must name `match_cut` and explain its purpose. If no match cut is needed, use `hard_cut` and state why.

## Product Category Aesthetic Defaults

| Category | Aesthetic emphasis | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Power Bank | movement, tactile carry, practical light, continuity | heroic energy beams or fake battery data |
| Wireless Charger | quiet placement, alignment, bedside/desk calm | floating devices or magical charging |
| Audio | personal boundary, controlled focus, observed movement | invented sound visualization or impossible fit |
| Dock / Hub | organized complexity, clear connection map, desk precision | extra ports or unverified compatibility |
| Switch Accessories | portability, social energy, durable carry | game UI or device details not in reference |
| Cables | tactile bend, connection ritual, organization | exaggerated elasticity or unsupported durability |
| Screen Protectors | touch, clarity, clean surface, careful installation | catastrophic damage unless approved |
| Creator Accessories | setup-to-creation flow, stable framing, human control | automatic quality claims or fake production output |

## Aesthetic QA

- Is the product naturally embedded in the selected world?
- Does each camera move have a product or narrative reason?
- Are material, color, and light supported by references?
- Does the visual arc move from attention to understanding to desire to recall?
- Are product and brand worlds visually related without becoming identical?
- Are all product states and transitions physically plausible?
